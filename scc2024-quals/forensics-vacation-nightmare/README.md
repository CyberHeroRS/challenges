#  Vacation Nightmare

**Author** : Marko Gordic  
**Difficulty**: <font color='red'>Hard</font>  
**Description**: CyberHero recently went through a tough breakup. He decided to take a vacation and intentionally left all his tech devices behind. During his absence, his ex-partner broke into his house and accessed his personal laptop to snoop around and potentially exfiltrate some files. As she was navigating through the files, numerous PowerShell windows suddenly began popping up across the screen, sowing confusion. To her dismay, she observed some of CyberHero's important files being encrypted before her eyes. Upon discovering a breach, CyberHero found his important files encrypted. He provided a network capture for analysis, believing it contained clues to the unauthorized access and activities on his laptop. He urgently requested help to decrypt a critical file affected by the incident.
