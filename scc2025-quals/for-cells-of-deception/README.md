# Forensics - Cells of Deception

**Author** : Marko Gordic  
**Difficulty**: <font color='green'>Medium</font>  
**Description**: Recently, your organization discovered unusual behavior on multiple systems. After a thorough investigation, the team traced a series of suspicious activities back to an Excel document found on a compromised workstation. Alarmed by unauthorized network connections and unexpected file modifications, forensic experts quickly isolated the file for closer examination. They believe its contents may hold vital clues about the nature of the intrusion. Now, it’s up to you to examine every cell, uncover hidden clues, and piece together the mystery behind this attack.
